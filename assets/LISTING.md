# Recipe listing

**Title:** What's Powering Britain

**Short description:**
Gas, wind, sunshine or a cable from France? See where Britain's electricity is coming from, at a glance.

**Long description:**
A calm, stacked chart of Great Britain's electricity by source: nuclear, wind, solar, bio/hydro, imports and gas. A bold line on top shows total demand. Watch the evening peak climb, the solar hump rise at lunchtime and the wind come and go, without opening an app.

- Choose how far back to look: 6 hours to 3 days
- Top 4 sources right now, or a summary with GW, peak, low-carbon % and carbon intensity
- GW or % share, legend on or off
- Free public data (Elexon, Sheffield Solar PV_Live, Carbon Intensity API). No keys, no accounts.
